# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume they are trying to help. Criticise the code, not
the person who wrote it.

## The longer version

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. Expected behaviour:

- Be welcoming to people with different experience levels, hardware and
  languages. Issues and pull requests in Vietnamese or English are both fine.
- Take feedback on your patch as feedback on the patch. Give it the same way.
- Focus on what is best for the people using this setup.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests, in commit messages, and
anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/anvu69/windows11-dev-poweruser/security/advisories/new).
It is the private channel this repository has, and it works for conduct reports
as well as vulnerabilities. Reports stay confidential.

The maintainer will respond as they judge appropriate: a warning, removing a
comment, or blocking an account. There is one maintainer, so expect a human
timescale rather than a process.
